# Collection Report: certifications.csv

Package: Package006_Skills_and_Training
Dataset: `datasets/certifications.csv`
Collection date: 2026-07-24
Researcher: automated research agent (WebSearch only; WebFetch to .gov.in/.ac.in/.nic.in blocked in this environment)

## Methodology

- Research was conducted exclusively via WebSearch (WebFetch to `.gov.in`, `.ac.in`, and `.nic.in` domains returns HTTP 403 in this environment and was not used). Search-result snippets from government and quasi-government domains (ncvet.gov.in, nsdcindia.org, nqr.gov.in, nielit.gov.in/nielit.in, msde.gov.in, swayam.gov.in, nptel.ac.in) were treated as Tier 1 evidence when the snippet text directly supported a claim; official vendor pages (Cisco, Microsoft, AWS, Google, Autodesk, Siemens, FANUC, ABB, Schneider Electric, Certiport) were treated as Tier 3; third-party aggregator/blog pages were used only to corroborate figures (cost, validity) when no higher-tier source surfaced one, and are flagged in `notes` accordingly.
- 30 real, individually named certifications were researched, spanning the full set of categories requested: NSDC/Skill India generic certification, an NCVET-regulated Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) mechanism, four Sector Skill Council qualifications (ASDC, IT-ITeS SSC NASSCOM, ESSCI, Capital Goods Skill Council), two more SSC qualifications (Healthcare SSC's General Duty Assistant, Beauty & Wellness SSC's Trainee Beautician), two NIELIT qualifications (O Level, CCC) plus a third (A Level), Cisco CCNA, two Microsoft certifications (Azure Fundamentals AZ-900, Microsoft Office Specialist), AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner, two Google credentials (IT Support Professional Certificate, Digital Garage), Autodesk Certified User – AutoCAD, Siemens SMSCP, a FANUC robot-operator training/certification pathway, an ABB Robotics customer-certification course, Schneider Electric's EcoXpert partner certification, a PMKVY Short-Term Training skill certificate, NPTEL certification, SWAYAM certification, IIT Bombay's Spoken Tutorial FOSS certification, Telangana's TASK certification program, Andhra Pradesh's APSSDC certification, a Tally Prime/Tally.ERP9 vendor certification, and a Red Hat–NSDC (Skill India Digital Hub) RHCSA pathway.
- Each qualification-pack-based row cites its official QP code (e.g. ASC/Q1411, SSC/Q2212, ELE/Q4601, CSC/Q0115, HSS/Q5101, BWS/Q0108) where one was found, to make the row independently verifiable against the National Qualification Register (nqr.gov.in).
- `certification_id` values are freshly generated UUIDv4s. `collection_date` is `2026-07-24` and `verification_status` is `VST-NEEDS_REVIEW` for every row, per instructions.
- The WebSearch tool's per-session query budget was exhausted (200/200) before every last-mile fee/level lookup could be independently double-checked (notably for the Beauty & Wellness SSC Trainee Beautician QP's exact NSQF level and fee, and a few vendor exam-fee reconciliations). Those specific cells are marked `PENDING_VERIFICATION` rather than guessed.

## Fill rates

- Total data rows: 30 (plus 1 header row = 31 lines in the CSV).
- Cells containing the exact bare string `PENDING_VERIFICATION`: **32** across the 30-row × 15-column (450-cell) dataset — i.e., roughly 7% of all cells. All were verified programmatically post-write to contain the exact bare string with nothing appended (a Python validation pass re-read the CSV and flagged any cell where `PENDING_VERIFICATION` appeared as a substring without being the entire cell value; the only such occurrence was inside a `notes` explanatory sentence, which is expected and correct usage — not a mis-tagged data cell).
- Breakdown of `PENDING_VERIFICATION` cells by column:
  - `validity_period`: 13 rows (mostly Sector Skill Council QP-based qualifications and several vendor/partner programs where no renewal interval was published in the sources reviewed)
  - `cost_indicator`: 11 rows (chiefly the industrial-automation vendor programs — Siemens SMSCP, FANUC, ABB, Schneider EcoXpert — plus CGSC, APSSDC, TASK, Beauty & Wellness SSC, NIELIT O/A Level, and the Red Hat–NSDC pathway, where no public fee schedule was found)
  - `nsqf_level`: 4 rows (TASK, APSSDC's own certificate layer, Beauty & Wellness SSC Trainee Beautician, Red Hat–NSDC RHCSA pathway)
  - `recognition_level`: 2 rows (FANUC India Academy pathway, ABB Robotics India customer certification) — India-specific formal recognition scope for these two industrial-automation vendor programs could not be confirmed distinct from their globally documented parent programs.
- `confidence_score` range across all 30 rows: **50–78** (never above 85, per instructions). Tier-1-sourced government/SSC/NIELIT/PMKVY/NPTEL/SWAYAM rows generally scored 72–78; vendor/industry certifications sourced mainly through third-party cost aggregators (Cisco, Microsoft, AWS, Autodesk, Tally) scored 55–65; the least-documented industrial-automation vendor rows (ABB, FANUC, Siemens SMSCP, Schneider EcoXpert) scored 50–58 to reflect the higher share of unconfirmed fields.

## Notable findings

- **NSQF-alignment is uneven and version-dependent even within a single issuer.** NIELIT's "O Level" qualification is registered on the National Qualification Register under multiple distinct entries at both NSQF Level 4 and Level 5 (differing by course code and notional hours), while CCC is consistently NSQF Level 3 and A Level is NSQF Level 6. This is documented in the row's `nsqf_level` field and `notes` rather than picking one figure arbitrarily.
- **PMKVY-funded training is free to the candidate and can pay the candidate a completion incentive** (approx. INR 8,000 under PMKVY 4.0) rather than charging a fee — this is a distinctive and easy-to-miss feature of the government scheme relative to vendor certifications, most of which charge USD 90–330 per exam attempt.
- **Global vendor foundational certifications increasingly carry no expiry**, in contrast to their advanced-tier siblings: Microsoft's AZ-900 (Azure Fundamentals) is explicitly lifetime-valid (the only Azure certification with no renewal requirement), while Cisco's CCNA and AWS's Cloud Practitioner both require recertification every 3 years.
- **Sector Skill Council (NCVET/NSDC) Qualification Packs consistently land at NSQF Level 4** for the entry-level trade roles researched (ASDC's Automotive Service Technician, NASSCOM SSC's Domestic Data Entry Operator, ESSCI's Field Technician – Computing & Peripherals, CGSC's CNC Operator – Turning, Healthcare SSC's General Duty Assistant), suggesting Level 4 is the modal entry point for India's frontline vocational qualifications — useful as a cross-check for other rows in this package.
- **Industrial-automation OEM certifications (Siemens SMSCP, FANUC, ABB, Schneider EcoXpert) are the weakest-documented tier in this dataset.** Their global program structures are well documented on corporate sites, but India-specific delivery details (whether Indian training centers issue the same globally-recognized credential, what it costs locally, and how long it remains valid) were not found in the sources reviewed within the available search budget. These four rows carry the lowest confidence scores (50–58) and the highest concentration of `PENDING_VERIFICATION` cells in the dataset.
- **State skill missions (TASK in Telangana, APSSDC in Andhra Pradesh) function primarily as facilitators/subsidizers of third-party and NSQF-aligned certifications** rather than issuing a single standardized credential of their own — this made `nsqf_level`, `validity_period`, and `cost_indicator` genuinely hard to pin to one figure for these two rows, reflected honestly via `PENDING_VERIFICATION` rather than an invented single answer.

## Conflicts encountered and how they were resolved

1. **Microsoft Office Specialist validity period**: some sources describe MOS certificates as having lifelong validity; others state that the MOS Excel Expert track specifically expires after 2 years. Rather than pick one, `validity_period` is marked `PENDING_VERIFICATION` with the conflict explained in `notes`.
2. **AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner exam fee**: sources disagree between USD 100 and USD 139 for the exam voucher (aws.amazon.com itself was not directly fetchable in this environment). Both figures are reported in `cost_indicator` with the discrepancy flagged in `notes`; the row is not marked `PENDING_VERIFICATION` because a qualitative bracket ("Paid — moderate") is still defensible, but the exact number is caveated.
3. **Spoken Tutorial exam fee**: the project's own process page cites INR 299/699 + GST (student/faculty), while at least one partner-institute page cites INR 200/500 + GST. Both figures are reported with the conflict noted.
4. **Cisco CCNA and Microsoft AZ-900 pricing**: neither vendor's own live pricing page was directly fetchable in this environment (WebFetch to non-.gov domains was not attempted per task scope, and WebSearch snippets came from third-party cost-aggregator blogs rather than cisco.com/microsoft.com). These are flagged in `notes` as indicative rather than vendor-confirmed.
