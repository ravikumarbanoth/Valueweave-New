## Overview

India has **1 doctor for every 1,500+ people** in rural areas — and even fewer diagnostic facilities. A patient in Medak or Khammam district needing a basic blood panel or ultrasound typically travels 20–40 km to the nearest town, losing half a day and paying ₹200–₹400 in transport alone.

Rural diagnostic centres do not compete with corporate hospitals. They serve a completely different need: accessible, affordable, fast results for the 60% of India that still lives beyond the reach of organised healthcare.

<Callout type="info">
  Diagnostic reports must be digitally signed. Invest in HMIS software from the beginning — it will save you compliance headaches and enable tele-radiology tie-ups.
</Callout>

---

## Market Opportunity

**Population served per diagnostic centre** in rural Telangana: 1 centre per 50,000–80,000 people. The WHO-recommended ratio is 1 centre per 10,000.

**Diagnostic market CAGR in India**: 14–16% (Tier-2 and Tier-3 growing faster than metros due to saturation effects in cities).

**Government tailwind**: PMJAY empanelment gives you a contracted patient base of all eligible card holders in your catchment — in Medak district alone, that is over 1.5 lakh PMJAY beneficiaries.

| Test | Price | PMJAY Rate |
|---|---|---|
| CBC (Complete Blood Count) | ₹150–₹250 | ₹120 |
| LFT (Liver Function Test) | ₹300–₹500 | ₹220 |

**Revenue at 20 patients/day (mix of walk-in + PMJAY)**:

- Average bill per patient: ₹350–₹500
- Monthly revenue: ₹2.1L–₹3L
- Monthly operational costs: ₹80K–₹1.2L
- **Net monthly income**: ₹90K–₹1.8L

## Skills Required

- **Lab Technician (DMLT/BMLT)**: core operator — handles sample collection and processing
- **Pathologist (Consulting)**: signs off on reports — can be a retainer arrangement (₹10K–₹20K/month part-time)
- **Radiologist (Tele-Radiology)**: for X-ray and USG reporting — tele-radiology platforms offer pay-per-report pricing
- **Front Desk / Receptionist**: patient registration, HMIS data entry, payment collection

---

## Team Required

Minimum viable team for a basic lab:

- 1 DMLT Lab Technician (full-time)
- 1 Receptionist/helper (full-time)
- 1 Pathologist (part-time / retainer)
- 1 Sample Collection Agent (mobile, for nearby villages — can be part-time initially)

## Government Support

### Ayushman Bharat PMJAY Empanelment

- **Benefit**: guaranteed patient volume + government reimbursements
- **Process**: apply through State Health Agency (SHA) Telangana or AP
- **Turnaround**: 3–6 months for inspection and empanelment

### NHM Free Diagnostics Initiative

Government PHCs and CHCs refer patients to empanelled labs for free tests — your centre can be empanelled to service these referrals.

### PM Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY)

DMLT training is included under PMKVY — you may be able to recruit subsidised-trained staff or train your own team at low cost.

<Callout type="tip">
  Apply for NABL accreditation after your first year of operations. It takes 6–12 months but immediately qualifies you for corporate hospital referral contracts and better insurance rates.
</Callout>

## Risks

1. **Quality failures**: an incorrect diagnostic report can cause serious patient harm and legal liability. Invest in internal QC protocols from day one.
2. **Doctor dependency**: if your consulting pathologist resigns, operations stall. Build relationships with at least two pathologists.
3. **Cash flow in early months**: PMJAY reimbursements have 30–60 day payment cycles. Maintain 3 months operating capital before opening.
4. **Competition from franchise labs**: national lab chains are expanding into Tier-2 towns through franchises. Your advantage is hyper-local access and trust — lean into it.
