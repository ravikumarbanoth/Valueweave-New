# Knowledge Graph Vision

ValueWeave's long-term intelligence layer should be powered by a human-readable and AI-readable knowledge graph.

## Goal

Connect districts, industries, skills, opportunities, resources, manufacturing capabilities, people, institutions, and capital into one navigable economic graph.

## Why It Matters

- Users can move from local context to practical next steps.
- Admins can manage content without duplicating structures.
- Search, SEO, GEO, and future AI systems can understand relationships.
- Recommendations can be explained instead of feeling arbitrary.

## Graph Layers

### Public Knowledge

Content visible to all users:
- District profiles
- Industry pages
- Skills
- Schemes
- Resources
- Manufacturing guides
- Scaling resources

### User Knowledge

Permissioned user-owned data:
- Profiles
- Skills
- Interests
- Saved modules
- Collaboration intent
- Opportunity posts

### Operational Knowledge

Admin and platform signals:
- Demand signals
- Analytics
- Content gaps
- Search intent
- Performance signals

### AI-Readable Knowledge

Structured summaries and relationships prepared for future AI systems:
- Key takeaways
- Entity metadata
- Relationship edges
- FAQs
- Source notes
- Confidence indicators

## Guardrails

- Do not add AI before the knowledge base is reliable.
- Keep source visibility and admin review in the loop.
- Avoid black-box recommendations for high-stakes decisions.
- Keep user data permissioned and separate from public knowledge.
