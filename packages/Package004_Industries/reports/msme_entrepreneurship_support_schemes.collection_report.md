# Collection Report: MSME & Entrepreneurship Support Schemes and Bodies
Package004_Industries_and_Livelihoods v1.0.0 — Telangana (TG) & Andhra Pradesh (AP)

## Methodology

- Research performed via WebSearch only. WebFetch to .gov.in/.ac.in/Wikipedia domains was confirmed blocked (HTTP 403) at session start, so no direct primary-source fetches were possible; all findings rest on WebSearch result snippets and the AI-generated summaries WebSearch returns, which themselves draw on a mix of official and secondary/aggregator sites (news portals, consultancy blogs, scheme-tracker sites).
- For each of the 5 mandated national schemes (PMEGP, Stand-Up India, PMMY, Startup India, NSDC) and the mandated TG bodies (T-IDEA/T-PRIDE, TS-iPASS, WE-HUB, TASK, TSIIC) and AP bodies (APIIC, AP Innovation Society, AP MSME policy), searches were run to (a) confirm the administering body, (b) confirm target beneficiaries and support offered with figures, (c) check for renames/reorganizations post-2024 (given known political transitions: Telangana Congress government from Dec 2023, Andhra Pradesh TDP-led coalition from June 2024), and (d) identify an authoritative official website.
- Three additional nationally-relevant entities were added beyond the mandated list because they are core, closely adjacent MSME-support instruments repeatedly surfacing in searches and directly relevant to "industries and livelihoods": CGTMSE (credit guarantee backbone underlying most MSME lending), PM Vishwakarma (traditional-trades/artisan livelihoods scheme, a strong thematic fit for this package), and the Telangana MSME Policy 2024 / AP MSME Policy 4.0 (current umbrella state policies layered on top of the older T-IDEA/APIIC-era schemes).
- District Industries Centre (DIC) was collapsed into a single generic row per task instruction, describing its role/mandate rather than enumerating district-level offices (33 in Telangana, 26 in AP).
- All rows were capped at confidence 70-82 (within the instructed 85-88 ceiling) because no row could be corroborated by a direct primary-source fetch this session — figures rest on WebSearch-aggregated secondary reporting.

## Renames / Reorganizations Found

1. **TS-iPASS → TG-iPASS**: The Telangana single-window industrial clearance system's branding shifted from "TS" (Telangana State) to "TG" (Telangana) prefix, matching a broader state convention shift also seen below. Both terms still circulate; ipass.telangana.gov.in is now TG-iPASS branded. Underlying 2014 Act and process are unchanged.
2. **TSIIC → TGIIC**: Telangana State Industrial Infrastructure Corporation is now branded TGIIC (Telangana Industrial Infrastructure Corporation). tgiic.telangana.gov.in appears to be the current primary domain; tsiic.telangana.gov.in remains live/redirecting. TGIIC was listed on NSE as a High Value Debt Listed Entity in Dec 2024. A contested opposition allegation (2025) about converting TGIIC into a public limited company to mortgage land was noted but not verified as fact — flagged, not incorporated as confirmed change.
3. **Telangana MSME Policy 2024** (launched Sept 18, 2024 by CM Revanth Reddy) is a new Congress-government policy layered on top of the older (2015-era) T-IDEA/T-PRIDE framework, with different headline subsidy figures (25% capital subsidy capped at Rs 30 lakh vs. T-IDEA's cited 15%/Rs 20 lakh). **Unresolved conflict**: sources do not clarify whether MSME Policy 2024 supersedes T-IDEA for MSME-category claims or the two operate in parallel with different scope. Flagged PENDING_VERIFICATION in the CSV; both schemes are kept as separate rows since neither source set explicitly states T-IDEA has been discontinued.
4. **AP MSME and Entrepreneurship Development Policy 4.0 (2024-2029)** and **AP Innovation & Startup Policy 4.0 (2024-2029)**: both are "4.0" iterations, i.e., current AP state policies under the TDP-led (Naidu) government, distinct from earlier YSRCP-era "3.0"-type policies. No explicit APIIC rename was confirmed as *current* — an older APIIC objectives document references a planned name "Corporation for Industrial Infrastructure in Andhra Pradesh (CIAP)" as a legal/incorporation clause, but APIIC continues to be the operative name across current government sources; this is flagged PENDING_VERIFICATION rather than treated as a completed rename.
5. **NSDC 2.0**: NSDC underwent a governance overhaul from August 2023 (new board, revised mandate) branded informally as "NSDC 2.0," but the legal entity name (National Skill Development Corporation) did not change.
6. Broader political context confirmed via search: AP's TDP-led government (from June 2024) has been reversing YSRCP-era scheme renames generally (e.g., YSR Bharosa → NTR Bharosa, YSRUHS → NTRUHS), consistent with prior ValueWeave package findings on AP scheme-rename patterns. No MSME/industries-specific YSR→NTR rename was located in this search pass; the AP MSME/Innovation policies found are versioned ("4.0") rather than renamed with a leader's name, so the pattern did not directly recur in this domain — but this should be re-checked if a future search surfaces an "NTR"-branded MSME/industries scheme.

## Conflicts Resolved / Judgment Calls

- Where sources gave slightly different subsidy figures (e.g., T-IDEA's capital subsidy variously reported), the most consistently repeated figure was used and the alternate figure noted in the row's `notes` field rather than silently dropped.
- DIC was represented as one generic national row (jurisdiction "National") rather than separate TG/AP rows or 60+ district rows, per task instruction, with a note clarifying it is administered per-state and applies identically in both TG and AP.
- Stand-Up India's reported loan-ceiling "revamp" (to Rs 2 crore) was treated as an in-progress announcement, not yet folded into the core support_offered_summary as a confirmed figure — the current Rs 10 lakh-Rs 1 crore range was kept as primary, with the proposed change flagged in notes.

## Known Gaps / PENDING_VERIFICATION Items

- No row in this dataset could be corroborated via direct primary-source (.gov.in) fetch this session; all confidence scores are capped at 70-82 accordingly.
- T-IDEA vs. Telangana MSME Policy 2024 relationship (supersession vs. parallel operation) is unresolved.
- APIIC vs. "Corporation for Industrial Infrastructure in Andhra Pradesh (CIAP)" naming is unresolved — likely a dormant legal provision, not an active rename, but not confirmed either way.
- Exact current CGTMSE guarantee-coverage percentage tiers (by loan size/category) were not itemized precisely.
- Startup India's reported 2026 framework expansions (turnover cap to Rs 200 crore, Deep Tech category, cooperative societies eligibility) come from secondary aggregator sites, not a primary DPIIT circular.
- Stand-Up India's reported loan-ceiling increase to Rs 2 crore is an announcement-stage item pending confirmation on standupmitra.in.
- No official DIC-specific website exists (function is described across MSME ministry and individual state/district portals); the URL used (dcmsme.gov.in) is the closest national-level reference, not a single canonical DIC portal.
