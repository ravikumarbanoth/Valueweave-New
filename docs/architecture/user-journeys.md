# User Journeys

This document captures future user journeys for ValueWeave's Digital Economic Infrastructure architecture.

## Entrepreneur Journey

1. Idea
2. Discover Opportunity
3. Develop Skills
4. Build Team
5. Start Manufacturing
6. Scale
7. Export
8. Mentor Others

## Student Journey

1. Explore districts and local industries.
2. Identify practical skills needed by those industries.
3. Follow readiness paths and training opportunities.
4. Join internships, apprenticeships, or industrial visits.
5. Build a collaborator profile.
6. Join or start a local opportunity.

## Local Business Journey

1. Understand district demand and nearby industries.
2. Identify operational gaps.
3. Find training, mentors, suppliers, and collaborators.
4. Use manufacturing guidance to improve production.
5. Use scaling resources for quality, logistics, export, and automation.

## Manufacturer Journey

1. Discover product opportunities.
2. Plan factory setup and machinery.
3. Source raw materials and suppliers.
4. Build production processes.
5. Improve quality and compliance.
6. Scale through automation, ERP, logistics, and export support.

## Mentor / Expert Journey

1. Create or maintain a trusted profile.
2. Attach expertise to skills, industries, districts, and modules.
3. Support founders and students.
4. Help mature businesses become ecosystem anchors.

## Future AI Journey

AI should assist only after the platform has reliable structured data.

Future AI journeys may include:
- District advisor
- Manufacturing advisor
- Readiness advisor
- Scale advisor

No AI behavior should be added before safety, source, and permission boundaries are clear.
